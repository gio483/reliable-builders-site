If you've started pricing a kitchen remodel in San Diego, you've probably noticed the numbers are all over the place. One contractor says $25,000. Another says $70,000. So what's the real answer?

Here's the honest version: **most full kitchen remodels in San Diego land between $30,000 and $60,000.** Where you land inside that range comes down to a few big choices.

## Where the money actually goes

Think of your kitchen budget in five buckets:

- **Cabinets** — usually the biggest single cost. Stock cabinets are cheapest, semi-custom is the sweet spot, and full custom is top of the range.
- **Countertops** — quartz and quartzite are the most popular. Price depends on the material and how much of it you need.
- **Labor** — demo, plumbing, electrical, and the skilled install work. This is where doing it right matters most.
- **Tile and backsplash** — a smaller line item, but bad tile work is the first thing guests notice.
- **Appliances, fixtures, and lighting** — easy to control, since you choose the price point.

## What pushes the price up

Three things move the number the most:

1. **Changing the layout.** Keeping plumbing and walls where they are keeps costs down. Moving the sink or taking out a wall adds real money — sometimes worth it, sometimes not.
2. **Custom cabinetry.** Beautiful, but it's the fastest way to climb from $40K to $60K.
3. **Structural surprises.** Older San Diego homes sometimes hide old wiring or plumbing that has to be brought up to code.

## How to get more kitchen for your money

You don't need the most expensive everything. A few smart moves:

- **Spend on cabinets and counters, save on the backsplash.** That's what people see and touch every day.
- **Keep the layout if it already works.** Moving plumbing is often the least visible way to blow your budget.
- **Pick a mid-range quartz.** The difference between a $60/sq ft and a $120/sq ft slab is often taste, not quality.

## The number that should worry you

If a contractor gives you a kitchen quote that's thousands below everyone else, be careful. In our experience, a lowball number usually means one of two things: they've left something out, or the "surprises" will show up once you've already committed.

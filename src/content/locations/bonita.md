Bonita is a bit of a hidden gem in the South Bay — a green, low-key enclave tucked into the Lower Sweetwater Valley, close enough to Chula Vista to be convenient but a world apart in feel. Horse trails, larger lots, open space, and a quiet semi-rural character make it one of the more distinctive places we work. We remodel kitchens, bathrooms, and ADUs here for homeowners who've usually been in the house a while and want the finish level to finally match the land it sits on.

One detail that trips people up: even though Bonita sits right against the city of Chula Vista, Bonita itself is **unincorporated**. There's no City of Bonita. The community falls under the County of San Diego, which means its permitting, zoning, and ADU rules follow the County — not Chula Vista's. That's one of the first things we confirm before quoting any Bonita project, because getting it wrong at the start can stall a job for weeks.

## Permitting in Bonita

Because Bonita is unincorporated, permits run through the **County of San Diego's Planning & Development Services**, not the City of Chula Vista — a distinction that matters, since the two jurisdictions have different processes. We handle the entire County submittal: application, plan set, and inspections, so you're never the one navigating it.

A kitchen or bathroom remodel that stays within the existing footprint — new cabinets, counters, tile, fixtures, lighting, and like-for-like plumbing and electrical — is the more straightforward track. Anything structural or anything that expands the home takes more: removing a wall to open a kitchen (we confirm whether it's load-bearing and engineer a beam if needed), relocating plumbing, adding square footage, or building an ADU. Bonita's semi-rural lots can also bring their own wrinkles — septic capacity, well considerations, floodplain proximity near the Sweetwater River in spots, and grading on sloped parcels. We identify which of those apply to your specific Bonita property before we quote, so the County permit path is built into the plan rather than discovered halfway through.

## ADUs in Bonita

ADUs are a natural fit for Bonita, and they're one of the most common reasons homeowners here call us — often for an aging parent, an adult child, or a guest house that makes sense on a larger parcel. Bonita's bigger lots are genuinely well-suited to a detached unit that sits comfortably on the land rather than crammed against a setback.

Because Bonita is unincorporated, your ADU follows **California's statewide ADU laws as administered by the County of San Diego**. That framework now allows most single-family lots to add a detached ADU, convert an existing structure, or add a junior ADU, within the state's size and setback rules, and recent state changes have smoothed the path considerably. On Bonita's larger properties, septic capacity and utility runs often matter more to the feasibility than lot size does — which is why we won't promise specific dimensions or costs before we've studied your actual parcel. That feasibility check comes first on every Bonita ADU; then we handle the County permit set, engineering, and build so the finished unit is legal, financeable, and genuinely livable.

For a lot of San Diego families, the conversation starts the same way. A parent is getting older. Living far away doesn't feel safe anymore. But a nursing facility feels like too much, too soon — and honestly, nobody wants it.

An ADU can be the answer. It lets your parents live **right in your backyard**: close enough to help, private enough to keep their independence, and safe enough to give everyone peace of mind.

If that's where you are, here's how to think it through — the honest way.

## Why families choose an ADU for parents

A backyard ADU solves a problem that's both practical and emotional:

- **They stay close.** You're a few steps away if something happens — but they still have their own front door.
- **They keep their dignity.** It's their home, not a spare room. That independence matters more than people realize.
- **You save money over time.** Compared to years of assisted living, an ADU can be the smarter long-term move.
- **It adds value to your property.** A legal, well-built unit is a real asset, even after it's no longer needed for this.

This is one of the most common reasons we build ADUs, and it's one of the most rewarding. You're not just adding square footage. You're keeping a family together.

## Design it for how they'll actually live

An ADU for aging parents isn't just a small home. It's a small home built with their needs in mind. A few things worth planning for:

- **No-step or low-step entry.** Getting in and out should be easy and safe, today and years from now.
- **Wider doorways and hallways.** Helpful if a walker or wheelchair ever enters the picture.
- **A safe bathroom.** Think grab bars, a walk-in or roll-in shower, and non-slip flooring.
- **Good lighting.** Bright, even light helps aging eyes and prevents falls.
- **Simple, single-level living.** Everything on one floor, easy to move around.

You don't have to make it feel like a hospital. Done right, these features just make a home that's comfortable for everyone — and they make it a better unit for the long run, too.

This is exactly why we lean so hard on the design stage. Because we work with a design partner, we can shape a layout around your parents' real daily life, not a generic floor plan.

## Detached ADU or garage conversion?

Both can work beautifully for parents. The right pick depends on your lot and budget.

- A **detached ADU** gives them a true, private, separate home with good light. These generally **start around $150,000**.
- A **garage conversion** usually **costs less**, since the shell already exists — a good option if the garage is the right size and in a spot that works.

If accessibility is a priority, a ground-level detached unit or a well-planned conversion both get you there. We'll look at your property and tell you honestly which one fits best.

## The part you can't skip: build it legal

When it's family, the temptation to "just do it quick and cheap" is strong. Please don't.

An unpermitted unit can cause real problems later — at resale, at refinance, or with the city. And when your parents are living in it, you want it **safe and up to code**: proper electrical, proper plumbing, proper heating and cooling, all inspected.

A permitted, engineered, inspected ADU is an asset. An off-the-books one is a liability. We only build them the right way, every time.

Here's some good news: California's statewide ADU laws have made it much easier to add a unit like this. More lots qualify than most people assume.

## What a real project includes

A proper ADU for your parents covers:

- **Feasibility and planning** — checking your setbacks, utilities, and what your lot allows before you spend on drawings.
- **Permits and engineering** — so it's legal, safe, and financeable.
- **Full utilities** — electrical, plumbing, and heating/cooling done to code.
- **A comfortable, accessible interior** — a real kitchen, a safe bathroom, and finishes that feel like home, not an add-on.
